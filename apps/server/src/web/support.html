CellMate Support

CellMate is a private, end-to-end encrypted messenger for companies: chats, groups, channels, voice and video calls.

Getting started

Your company admin gives you a company code, ID and password, or a QR invite. Open CellMate, enter them and sign in. New companies can register from the sign-in screen.

Common questions

  • Forgot your password? Ask your company admin to reset it.
  • New phone? Sign in on the new phone; your chats come back from the encrypted backup.
  • Someone is bothering you? Open their chat, tap their name, then Block or Report.
  • Delete your account? Settings > Delete account.
